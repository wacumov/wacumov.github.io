Privacy Policy

Mikhail Akopov built Word Haven app as a Commercial app. This app is provided by Mikhail Akopov and is intended for use as is.

This policy applies to all information collected or submitted using the app.

Information the App Collects

The app itself does not collect personal information and has no servers of its own. Your games, scores and settings stay on your device.

The app shows ads through the Appodeal SDK. Appodeal and its advertising partners may collect:

  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks;
  • device data: model, operating system version, language, time zone and screen size;
  • the IP address, which some ad networks may use to estimate the country or region;
  • ad data: which ads were shown and tapped.

They use this data to show ads, to personalise them, to measure how ads perform and to prevent fraud. If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to show you ads based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. For more details, see the Appodeal Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

You can allow or deny tracking when the app asks, and change it any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Where the law requires it, the app also asks for your consent to personalised ads. Without tracking you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Data Retention and Deletion

The app keeps no data on servers, so the developer holds nothing to delete. Appodeal and its partners keep and delete the data they collect under their own policies; to ask them for access or deletion, use the contacts in the Appodeal Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The current app version and later does not use any first or third party analytics other than the Apple App Analytics, which you may optionally opt into on your device. These are covered by Apple’s (Apple Inc.) privacy policy which can be found under the privacy settings on the iOS device. The key reason these analytics are used by the app is to be able to detect app crashes and fix them.
